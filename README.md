## 🔍 What Is open-spark-jev?

open-spark-jev is a **local decision model** inspired by TypeSafe’s Jev and System One, built on the advanced Qwen3 architecture and optimized for NVIDIA DGX Spark. In everyday terms, it's a smart assistant that helps your computer make quick, reliable decisions using AI—**all offline, right on your machine**. No internet connection required, no data leaving your computer, and no monthly fees.

Whether you're automating tasks, processing data, or building your own intelligent workflows, open-spark-jev gives you the power of a sophisticated AI engine without needing to be a programmer.
